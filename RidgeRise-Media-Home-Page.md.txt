FAQ
What does RidgeRise Media do? We're a performance marketing company focused on customer acquisition. We build digital campaigns that generate consumer demand and convert it into qualified inbound calls for businesses.


What is Pay Per Call? A performance model where advertising drives interested consumers to call, and qualified calls are connected with businesses that can serve them. Performance is measured at the call level. Real conversations, not clicks.


Where does your traffic come from? Two places: campaigns we build and manage ourselves through in house media buying, and approved publishers who meet our quality standards. We're transparent about the mix on any campaign.


How do you manage call quality? Quality starts with campaign design. Targeting, messaging, and qualification criteria are agreed upfront, and we continue with ongoing monitoring of sources, call behavior, and conversion performance.


How do we get started? Start a conversation with our team. We'll walk through who you want to reach, your campaign economics, and whether there's a fit before anything launches.
